Launcher v2
- res/font/inter_regular.ttf, inter_semibold.ttf, inter_display_light.ttf, inter_display_regular.ttf,
  inter_display_semibold.ttf (and the inter_ui.xml family): Inter 4.1 by Rasmus Andersson, SIL Open Font
  License 1.1 (https://github.com/rsms/inter), subset to Latin. v2's text face.
- res/font/hind_regular.ttf, hind_semibold.ttf: Hind, by the Indian Type Foundry, SIL Open Font License 1.1
  (https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Hind). v2's earlier text face, now unused.
- res/drawable-nodpi/v2_nord.png: the NordVPN mountain mark, cut from NordVPN's own app icon, for the top bar's VPN
  button on the owner's TV. A trademark of Nord Security; kept out of any upstream pull request.
- res/drawable/ic_v2_*.xml: drawn for this launcher.

- res/drawable-nodpi/fmt_dolby_vision.png, fmt_dolby_atmos.png, fmt_dolby.png: the Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos
  logos from Wikimedia Commons (Dolby_Vision_(logo).svg, Dolby_Atmos_(logo).svg), recoloured white; fmt_hdr10plus.png:
  the HDR10+ logo from hdr10plus.org (its drop shadow taken off), and fmt_hdr10.png the same artwork without the "+"
  (the "0" closed); trademarks of their owners, shown only to say which formats a title is in. fmt_dolby_vision_atmos.png is a compact Dolby lockup (the double-D with Vision over Atmos),
  put together from those two logos.
- res/drawable-nodpi/svc_*.png: the streaming services' logos (Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV, Channel 4,
  5, Paramount+ from TMDB's network logos; BBC iPlayer and ITVX from Wikipedia's logo files; Sky's own 2020 wordmark
  (svc_sky) from TMDB's company logos), trimmed, the
  dark ones drawn white for the dark screen; trademarks of their owners, shown only to say where a title streams.
