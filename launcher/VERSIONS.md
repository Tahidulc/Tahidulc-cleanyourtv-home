# Launcher versions

Each change to the launcher is a new version: `versionName` and `versionCode` in `app/build.gradle` go up by one
(2.50 is versionCode 250), the commit is tagged (`v2.50`), and a line goes here with what changed and which TVs
have it.

| Version | What changed | On |
|---|---|---|
| 2.49 | Starting point for version numbers. Live Centre: one scoreboard bar with the competition's logo, club crests, a blue score box and a clock counting minutes and seconds; stats and facts folding out of it (goalscorers with pictures, player of the match, formations, win chance before kick-off, detailed stats, form, table); goals, substitutions and line-ups folding out as they happen; goal videos for every LaLiga game; official highlights at full time, played inside it. Alerts: every logo and photo from its full-size original, larger crests and F1 team logos, Mercedes' star lifted on black, the full-time card naming its winner. Pop-up mode: OK reaches the app on the TCL. | TCL |
| 2.50 | Channel tuning from Home tiles and the TV guide: the helper is connected again before TiviMate opens (without it TiviMate only opened on the last channel); a tune gives up after 30 seconds instead of minutes, closes TiviMate's search if it is still up and says "Couldn't find …" rather than lifting onto another channel; the search is closed sooner after a pick. New, off unless a TV's theme sets `"channel_app": "kleva"`: channels tuned through Kleva TV's search (one card per channel), TiviMate when Kleva TV hasn't got it. Each tune's time is logged. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.51 | Ambient mode: a large thin clock, the date, the weather now and tomorrow, cards for the game on now (with its score) or the next one with its channel, the next F1 session and the camera's last motion, and today's results along the foot; it drifts each minute and dims after 23:00 so nothing burns in, and never keeps the screen on. Starts on Home after the theme's `"ambient_minutes"` without a key (off unless set; not while anything plays), and is also a screensaver (AmbientDream). | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.52 | Match Day on Home: on a day with a game that matters, the top of Home becomes it (crests, competition, countdown with each side's last five, channel and coverage time, then the live score and clock, then the result; Watch, Live Centre, Remind me). Manchester United first, then England, then Barcelona, Real Madrid, Bayern Munich, PSG, AC Milan, Inter and Juventus, then Premier League, Champions League, EFL Cup and FA Cup games live on British TV; from the morning until three hours after kick-off. For You, the boxes and the rail stay put: Down to For You, Up back, Back hides it for the day. Ambient mode: everything drifts each minute, swaps sides every five minutes, dims after five minutes and late at night, its hint goes after a minute, and after the theme's `"ambient_screensaver_minutes"` (10) the TV's screensaver takes over (the launcher's moving clock where the TV has no way to start it). | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.53 | UHD first, then HD: Match Day shows the UHD channel and its UHD logo (Sky's logo with its HD box redrawn as UHD) wherever the playlist has the UHD channel, and Watch and its loading screen go to that channel; TiviMate's search picks a channel's UHD version, else HD/FHD, before one with no mark or SD; Kleva TV the same. Alerts, the Live Centre, Home's tiles and the guide already put UHD first. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.54 | Match Day follows 3pm Saturday Premier League games too (the bigger clubs' first, Manchester United's always first): countdown, form, live score and clock, Live Centre, a reminder, and Soccer Saturday on Sky Sports News, which covers them live; no channel is shown for the game itself, as British TV does not show it. With several games still to come, the one starting first. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.55 | Match Day for Champions League games: Watch goes to the game's channel as the Sports page does (Sky's guide, UHD first, else the playlist through TiviMate or Kleva TV), named only when Sky's guide lists it; a game Sky's guide leaves out on an evening it lists the others is Prime Video's, shown as "Live on Prime Video" with an Open Prime Video button; where Sky has no picture for the game, the two clubs' colours split on a slant behind the crests. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.56 | Match Day's Watch button always names the channel it goes to ("Watch on TNT Sports 1", "Watch live on …"), however the channel was found, without a playlist's country prefix. | Not on a TV yet |
