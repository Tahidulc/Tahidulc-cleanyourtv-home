# Launcher versions

Each change to the launcher is a new version: `versionName` and `versionCode` in `app/build.gradle` go up by one
(2.50 is versionCode 250), the commit is tagged (`v2.50`), and a line goes here with what changed and which TVs
have it.

| Version | What changed | On |
|---|---|---|
| 2.49 | Starting point for version numbers. Live Centre: one scoreboard bar with the competition's logo, club crests, a blue score box and a clock counting minutes and seconds; stats and facts folding out of it (goalscorers with pictures, player of the match, formations, win chance before kick-off, detailed stats, form, table); goals, substitutions and line-ups folding out as they happen; goal videos for every LaLiga game; official highlights at full time, played inside it. Alerts: every logo and photo from its full-size original, larger crests and F1 team logos, Mercedes' star lifted on black, the full-time card naming its winner. Pop-up mode: OK reaches the app on the TCL. | TCL |
| 2.50 | Channel tuning from Home tiles and the TV guide: the helper is connected again before TiviMate opens (without it TiviMate only opened on the last channel); a tune gives up after 30 seconds instead of minutes, closes TiviMate's search if it is still up and says "Couldn't find …" rather than lifting onto another channel; the search is closed sooner after a pick. New, off unless a TV's theme sets `"channel_app": "kleva"`: channels tuned through Kleva TV's search (one card per channel), TiviMate when Kleva TV hasn't got it. Each tune's time is logged. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.51 | Ambient mode: a large thin clock, the date, the weather now and tomorrow, cards for the game on now (with its score) or the next one with its channel, the next F1 session and the camera's last motion, and today's results along the foot; it drifts each minute and dims after 23:00 so nothing burns in, and never keeps the screen on. Starts on Home after the theme's `"ambient_minutes"` without a key (off unless set; not while anything plays), and is also a screensaver (AmbientDream). | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.52 | Match Day on Home: on a day with a game that matters, the top of Home becomes it (crests, competition, countdown with each side's last five, channel and coverage time, then the live score and clock, then the result; Watch, Live Centre, Remind me). Manchester United first, then England, then Barcelona, Real Madrid, Bayern Munich, PSG, AC Milan, Inter and Juventus, then Premier League, Champions League, EFL Cup and FA Cup games live on British TV; from the morning until three hours after kick-off. For You, the boxes and the rail stay put: Down to For You, Up back, Back hides it for the day. Ambient mode: everything drifts each minute, swaps sides every five minutes, dims after five minutes and late at night, its hint goes after a minute, and after the theme's `"ambient_screensaver_minutes"` (10) the TV's screensaver takes over (the launcher's moving clock where the TV has no way to start it). | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.53 | UHD first, then HD: Match Day shows the UHD channel and its UHD logo (Sky's logo with its HD box redrawn as UHD) wherever the playlist has the UHD channel, and Watch and its loading screen go to that channel; TiviMate's search picks a channel's UHD version, else HD/FHD, before one with no mark or SD; Kleva TV the same. Alerts, the Live Centre, Home's tiles and the guide already put UHD first. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.54 | Match Day follows 3pm Saturday Premier League games too (the bigger clubs' first, Manchester United's always first): countdown, form, live score and clock, Live Centre, a reminder, and Soccer Saturday on Sky Sports News, which covers them live; no channel is shown for the game itself, as British TV does not show it. With several games still to come, the one starting first. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.55 | Match Day for Champions League games: Watch goes to the game's channel as the Sports page does (Sky's guide, UHD first, else the playlist through TiviMate or Kleva TV), named only when Sky's guide lists it; a game Sky's guide leaves out on an evening it lists the others is Prime Video's, shown as "Live on Prime Video" with an Open Prime Video button; where Sky has no picture for the game, the two clubs' colours split on a slant behind the crests. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.56 | Match Day's Watch button always names the channel it goes to ("Watch on TNT Sports 1", "Watch live on …"), however the channel was found, without a playlist's country prefix. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.57 | Match Day's picture when Sky has none for the game: the launcher makes one as a broadcaster would, each side's top scorer in the competition (ESPN's leaders) cut out in the club's kit (TheSportsDB), standing in front of the clubs' colours split on a slant, with light streaks; the colours alone when no cut-out is found. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.58 | Match Day's picture: TheSportsDB's artwork for the fixture (the two crests and the competition's mark) for every Champions League game, and for any other game Sky has no picture for; found by the clubs' names (tidied, "AS Roma" as "Roma"), all 18 of this week's Champions League games found. The Live Centre button is the Live Centre's own logo (LIVE on green, CENTRE on white) instead of the words. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.59 | Match Day's order: Manchester United; then Premier League, Champions League, EFL Cup and FA Cup games on UK TV; then 3pm Saturday Premier League games; then England; then Barcelona, Real Madrid, Bayern Munich, PSG, AC Milan, Inter and Juventus. Between two games level on that, the one with England or a followed club in it. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.60 | One search for everything: a magnifier in Home's top bar (or the remote's Search key) opens Search, with an on-screen keyboard, voice and the last six searches on the left. As you type, on the right: a top result, then what's on TV today and tomorrow on the guide's channels (Sky's guide, live first; OK tunes a programme on now or sets a reminder for one later), a club's or England's next games (ESPN, with the channel from Sky's guide, UHD first; OK opens the Live Centre once it's on, or sets a reminder), films and series in the apps on this TV that stream them in Britain (TMDB; OK opens the title in its app), BBC iPlayer's own results, and "Search YouTube for …". Back closes it. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.61 | Movies has a "Fits tonight" tab: films on the streaming apps installed on this TV (TMDB's UK providers for them) that end before bed if started now, each poster saying when it would end ("Ends 22:38") and the picture above saying how long that leaves ("22 min before bed"). Bed is the theme's "bedtime" (23:00 when not set); in the day the films run up to three hours, and with under 80 minutes to bed, the short ones. OK does what the page's other tabs do. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.62 | TV shows has a "Your shows" tab: every series watched on this TV in the last six months (in any app), each poster labelled from TMDB with what is next: a new episode out ("NEW · S4 E1"), episodes still to watch ("4 LEFT", or "DONE BY 23:15" when they all fit before bed), the next one's day ("E7 TOMORROW", "BACK 10 DEC"), or "NEXT SEASON TBC", with "S3 · 4 of 8" along the foot. New episodes first. OK on a series on one of the TV's streaming apps does what the page's other tabs do; on a series on none of them, the store on the TV that sells it (Prime Video, Apple TV, YouTube), else a note that it is not on the apps. TV shows opens on it when there is nothing to continue. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.63 | Pop-ups when something new comes out (theme "release_alerts", on unless set false): a new episode of a series watched on this TV in the last two months, out today or yesterday and not yet watched, and a new film arriving on the TV's own streaming apps (TMDB's UK releases on them, the two most popular a day). A black card with the title's logo, a red NEW EPISODE or NEW FILM label, the episode's still and its name. For a title on one of the TV's streaming apps it says "Released on Debrid Stream" and OK opens Debrid Stream's page for it, as Movies and TV shows do; a new episode of a series on none of the TV's apps says "Out today", and OK opens the store on the TV that sells it. Checked every two hours from 08:00 to 23:30, each title once; adb's test_release shows the latest. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.64 | Pop-ups over apps are black with white writing (theme "black_alerts", on unless set false): the goal, score and news lines on F1's black glass with the white versions of the logos; the goal, red card, full time, fight and release cards black with a charcoal score block; the line-up card's navy band and pale pages black with white names and numbers; the delivery, food and car cards with white writing, a white track and the brands' colours brightened; the camera card's glass black. On Home the alerts keep the score pills' glass. | Not on a TV yet |
| 2.65 | Your viewing: a chart button in Home's top bar opens what has played on this TV in the last seven days, from a new log of Android's media sessions (any app, Debrid Stream and live TV in TiviMate or Kleva TV included; music and the launcher left out), kept in the app's own files for a year and a half and never sent anywhere. The hours against the week before and day by day; the apps, each with its own logo; when, as a grid of the evenings (17:00 to 01:00) by day, with the busiest two hours; the five most watched films and series with their posters, time and episodes; and days in a row, the share after 21:00, the biggest day and live TV's hours. Back closes it. | Not on a TV yet |
